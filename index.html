Two directions for the new League Championship site

Both are clickable prototypes of the same five screens (Home, Schedule, Event hub with standings and bracket, Team, Leaderboard) built on the same sample season. Compare the look and feel; the features and content are identical.

1. Open a directionUse the site's own top navigation to move between the five screens. The Event hub also has working tabs.
2. Try it on your phoneOpen the same link on a phone, or narrow the browser, to see how the schedule and bracket collapse.
3. Choose look, not featuresTeams, players, scores and news are fictional sample data. Pick the palette, type and surface treatment you want to move forward with.

ABroadcast

The lolesports.com look with League Championship's own events and logos. Near-black canvas, electric cyan as the signal colour, condensed uppercase headings, three-column schedule hub, red LIVE pills and dark bracket boxes. Reads instantly as an official esports property.

CCrimson Circuit
